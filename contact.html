Common Questions

Is care really free?

Yes. All services are 100% covered by Georgia Medicaid for eligible children under 21. No copays or hidden fees.

How do I know if my child qualifies?

Contact us for a free consultation. If your child has Medicaid and complex medical needs, they likely qualify.

What is the GAPP program?

GAPP allows parents to become paid caregivers for their own medically fragile child. Learn more.

What areas do you serve?

We serve families within 50 miles of Atlanta, covering 10+ metro counties. See service area.
