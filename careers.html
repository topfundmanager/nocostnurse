Registered Nurse (RN)

Full-Time & Part-Time Available

Your Role

As a Registered Nurse with No Cost Nurse, you'll provide skilled nursing care to medically fragile children in their homes. You'll assess patient conditions, develop and implement care plans, administer medications and treatments, and serve as a clinical leader for the care team.

Responsibilities Include:

  • Comprehensive patient assessments and ongoing monitoring
  • Administration of medications, IV therapy, and specialized treatments
  • Ventilator and tracheostomy care for respiratory-dependent patients
  • G-tube and other enteral feeding management
  • Wound care and complex dressing changes
  • Care plan development and documentation
  • Family education and support
  • Coordination with physicians and interdisciplinary team

Qualifications:

  • Current Georgia RN license (or compact state license)
  • CPR/BLS certification (PALS preferred)
  • Minimum 1 year nursing experience (pediatric experience preferred)
  • Reliable transportation and valid driver's license
  • Compassionate, patient-centered approach

What We Offer:

  • Competitive hourly rates based on experience and case complexity
  • Flexible scheduling: days, nights, weekends available
  • Paid orientation and ongoing training
  • Supportive clinical leadership and 24/7 on-call support
  • Malpractice insurance coverage

Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN)

Full-Time & Part-Time Available

Your Role

As an LPN with No Cost Nurse, you'll provide hands-on nursing care to pediatric patients under RN supervision. You'll monitor patient conditions, administer medications, perform skilled procedures, and ensure the safety and comfort of the children in your care.

Responsibilities Include:

  • Vital signs monitoring and patient observation
  • Medication administration (oral, injectable, topical, inhaled)
  • Tracheostomy care including suctioning and routine tube care
  • G-tube feeding administration and site care
  • Wound care and dressing changes
  • Documentation and care reporting
  • Patient and family education
  • Collaboration with RN supervisors and care team

Qualifications:

  • Current Georgia LPN license (or compact state license)
  • CPR/BLS certification
  • Previous nursing experience (pediatric preferred but will train the right candidate)
  • Reliable transportation
  • Warm, patient, compassionate personality

What We Offer:

  • Competitive hourly pay
  • Flexible shifts to match your availability
  • Comprehensive orientation and pediatric training
  • Career advancement pathways (RN tuition assistance programs)
  • Supportive team environment

Certified Nursing Assistant (CNA)

Full-Time & Part-Time Available

Your Role

As a CNA with No Cost Nurse, you'll provide essential personal care and support to pediatric patients under nursing supervision. You're the hands that help children through their daily routines with patience, dignity, and genuine care.

Responsibilities Include:

  • Bathing, grooming, and personal hygiene assistance
  • Dressing and undressing support
  • Feeding assistance and meal preparation
  • Mobility assistance, transfers, and positioning
  • Toileting and incontinence care
  • Range of motion exercises as delegated
  • Vital signs monitoring
  • Companionship and engagement activities
  • Accurate documentation of care provided

Qualifications:

  • Current Georgia CNA certification
  • CPR/BLS certification
  • Experience with pediatric or special needs populations preferred
  • Reliable transportation
  • Genuine love for working with children
  • Patience, kindness, and positive attitude

What We Offer:

  • Competitive hourly rates
  • Consistent scheduling with long-term cases
  • Paid training and skills development
  • Pathway to nursing career (tuition assistance available)
  • Rewarding one-on-one patient relationships

Personal Care Aide (PCA)

Full-Time & Part-Time Available

Your Role

As a Personal Care Aide with No Cost Nurse, you'll provide non-medical assistance and companionship to children with special needs. You're often the consistent, friendly face that helps children feel safe and supported throughout their day.

Responsibilities Include:

  • Assistance with daily living activities
  • Meal preparation and feeding support
  • Light housekeeping related to patient care
  • Accompaniment to appointments and activities
  • Engagement in play, reading, and developmental activities
  • Monitoring and reporting changes to nursing staff
  • Following personalized care plans
  • Building trusting, supportive relationships with children and families

Qualifications:

  • High school diploma or equivalent
  • PCA certification or willingness to complete training
  • CPR certification (we can help you obtain this)
  • Previous experience with children or caregiving preferred
  • Reliable transportation
  • Warm, dependable, patient personality
  • Clear background check

What We Offer:

  • Hourly pay with consistent hours
  • Paid training and certification support
  • Flexible scheduling options
  • Meaningful work making a real difference
  • Supportive team and career growth opportunities
