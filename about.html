Who We Are

Your Partner in Pediatric Home Care

At No Cost Nurse, we understand that caring for a medically fragile child is both a profound responsibility and an extraordinary act of love. Our mission is to partner with Georgia families by providing professional, compassionate in-home nursing services at no cost to children under 21 who are covered by Medicaid.

We specialize in supporting children with complex medical needs—from infants requiring around-the-clock monitoring to young adults transitioning through critical developmental stages. Our team of licensed nurses, certified nursing assistants, and personal care aides delivers skilled medical care in the comfort and safety of your home, where healing happens best.

Every family we serve becomes part of our extended family. We don't just provide clinical care—we provide peace of mind, respite for exhausted parents, and the reassurance that comes from knowing your child is in expert, caring hands.

Our Commitment

What Sets No Cost Nurse Apart

100% Free Services

Every service we provide is fully covered by Georgia Medicaid for eligible children under 21. No copays, no deductibles, no hidden fees—ever.

GAPP Program Access

We help families enroll in the Georgia Pediatric Program (GAPP), allowing parents and family members to become paid caregivers for their own child.

Specialized Pediatric Team

Our nurses and caregivers receive specialized training in pediatric home health, including complex respiratory, nutritional, and medical interventions.

Care at Home

We bring hospital-quality care to your home—where your child is most comfortable, surrounded by family, and able to maintain normal routines.

Family-Centered Approach

We involve families in every aspect of care planning. Your input, preferences, and goals are at the center of everything we do.

24/7 Support

Our clinical team is available around the clock for emergencies and urgent questions. You're never alone when you partner with No Cost Nurse.

Our Expertise

Specialized Pediatric Training

Our clinical team maintains specialized training in pediatric home health, ensuring your child receives the highest quality care for even the most complex medical needs.

Our Clinical Capabilities Include:

  • Tracheostomy care and monitoring
  • Ventilator management
  • Oxygen therapy
  • Pulse oximetry monitoring
  • Nebulizer treatments
  • G-tube and J-tube feeding
  • Wound care and dressing changes
  • Medication administration
  • Seizure monitoring
  • Central line care

Every nurse and caregiver on our team holds current certifications in CPR, first aid, and pediatric emergency response. We invest in ongoing education to stay current with best practices in pediatric home care.
