Respiratory Support

Expert Care for Children with Complex Breathing Needs

For children who depend on respiratory equipment to breathe, every moment matters. Our nurses are specially trained in pediatric respiratory care, ensuring your child receives vigilant monitoring and immediate response to any changes in their breathing status.

Our Respiratory Support Services Include:

  • Tracheostomy Care: Complete trach management including suctioning, stoma care, tube changes, and emergency response
  • Ventilator Management: 24/7 monitoring of ventilator-dependent children, including circuit maintenance, alarm response, and weaning protocols as ordered by physicians
  • Oxygen Therapy: Administration and monitoring of supplemental oxygen via nasal cannula, face mask, or trach collar
  • Pulse Oximetry Monitoring: Continuous or spot-check oxygen saturation monitoring with immediate intervention protocols
  • Nebulizer Treatments: Administration of prescribed respiratory medications
  • Chest Physiotherapy: Airway clearance techniques to prevent respiratory complications

Our respiratory-trained nurses understand that parents need more than clinical expertise—they need peace of mind. We provide thorough education so you understand your child's equipment and can respond confidently between nursing shifts.

Skilled Nursing Services

Licensed Nurses Delivering Hospital-Quality Care at Home

Our team of Registered Nurses (RNs) and Licensed Practical Nurses (LPNs) brings advanced clinical skills directly to your child's bedside. Whether your child requires complex medical interventions or careful monitoring, our nurses are equipped to provide the highest standard of care.

Skilled Nursing Services Include:

  • Comprehensive Health Assessments: Regular evaluations of your child's overall health, growth, and developmental progress
  • Wound Care: Sterile dressing changes, wound measurement and documentation, infection prevention, and healing optimization
  • Intravenous (IV) Therapy: When prescribed, administration and monitoring of IV medications and fluids
  • Central Line Care: Maintenance, flushing, and dressing changes for PICC lines, ports, and other central venous access devices
  • Seizure Monitoring & Response: Observation, documentation, and emergency intervention for children with seizure disorders
  • Post-Surgical Care: Skilled nursing support during recovery from surgical procedures
  • Care Coordination: Communication with physicians, therapists, and other members of your child's healthcare team

Every nurse on our team has completed specialized training in pediatric home health and maintains current certifications in CPR, first aid, and pediatric emergency response.

Personal Care Assistance

Compassionate Support for Daily Living Activities

For children who need assistance with daily activities but may not require skilled nursing for every task, our Certified Nursing Assistants (CNAs) and Personal Care Aides (PCAs) provide compassionate, hands-on support that preserves your child's dignity and promotes independence.

Personal Care Services Include:

  • Bathing & Personal Hygiene: Assistance with bathing, oral care, hair care, and grooming
  • Dressing Assistance: Help with clothing, orthotics, and adaptive equipment
  • Toileting & Incontinence Care: Dignified assistance with toileting, diapering, and catheter care as delegated
  • Mobility Support: Safe transfers, positioning, wheelchair assistance, and ambulation support
  • Feeding Assistance: Support with oral feeding, including special diets and texture modifications
  • Range of Motion Exercises: Passive and active exercises as directed by therapists
  • Companionship & Engagement: Age-appropriate activities, reading, play, and social interaction

Our personal care team understands that they're not just assisting with tasks—they're becoming part of your child's world. We select caregivers who bring warmth, patience, and genuine connection to every interaction.

Therapy Coordination Services

Connecting Your Child with Specialized Therapies

While No Cost Nurse focuses on nursing and personal care, we recognize that many medically fragile children benefit from specialized therapies. Our team actively coordinates with therapy providers to ensure seamless, integrated care.

We Facilitate Access To:

  • Physical Therapy (PT): Improving mobility, strength, and motor function
  • Occupational Therapy (OT): Enhancing fine motor skills and daily living independence
  • Speech-Language Therapy (SLP): Supporting communication, swallowing, and feeding skills
  • Respiratory Therapy (RT): Specialized breathing treatments and equipment optimization
  • Developmental Therapy: Early intervention services for infants and toddlers

Our Coordination Role Includes:

  • Communicating therapy goals to caregiving staff
  • Reinforcing therapy exercises during care visits
  • Documenting progress and sharing observations with therapists
  • Scheduling care visits around therapy appointments
  • Ensuring equipment and positioning support therapy goals

Nutrition & Feeding Care

Supporting Healthy Growth Through Specialized Nutrition

Proper nutrition is foundational to every child's health, growth, and development. For children with feeding difficulties or those who receive nutrition through alternative methods, our nurses provide expert care to ensure your child thrives.

Nutrition Care Services Include:

  • Gastrostomy Tube (G-Tube) Care: Complete management including feeding administration, stoma care, tube maintenance, and troubleshooting
  • Nasogastric (NG) Tube Management: Insertion verification, feeding administration, and tube security
  • Jejunostomy (J-Tube) Care: Specialized care for children requiring post-pyloric feeding
  • Total Parenteral Nutrition (TPN): When prescribed, administration and monitoring of IV nutrition
  • Oral Feeding Support: Assistance with special diets, texture modifications, and positioning for safe oral intake
  • Intake & Output Monitoring: Careful documentation to ensure adequate hydration and nutrition
  • Weight & Growth Tracking: Regular measurements to monitor nutritional status

We work closely with your child's physicians and dietitians to follow prescribed feeding plans precisely while keeping you informed about your child's nutritional progress.

Medication Management

Safe, Accurate Administration of Your Child's Medications

Many medically fragile children require complex medication regimens—sometimes involving numerous medications given at precise times through various routes. Our licensed nurses ensure every dose is administered safely, accurately, and on schedule.

Medication Management Services Include:

  • Oral Medications: Pills, liquids, and dissolvable medications administered with proper technique
  • Injections: Subcutaneous and intramuscular injections including insulin, growth hormone, and other prescribed medications
  • Intravenous Medications: When ordered, IV push and infusion medications through appropriate access
  • Topical Applications: Medicated creams, ointments, patches, and wound treatments
  • Inhaled Medications: Nebulizer treatments, metered-dose inhalers, and other respiratory medications
  • Rectal & Other Routes: Emergency medications and other prescribed alternative routes
  • Medication Reconciliation: Maintaining accurate, updated medication lists and communicating changes to the care team

Safety Is Our Priority

Our nurses follow strict medication verification protocols, document every administration, monitor for side effects, and communicate promptly with physicians about any concerns.
