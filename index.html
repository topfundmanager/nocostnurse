About No Cost Nurse

Compassionate Care for Georgia's Most Precious Patients

At No Cost Nurse, we understand that caring for a medically fragile child is both a profound responsibility and an extraordinary act of love. Our mission is to partner with Georgia families by providing professional, compassionate in-home nursing services at no cost to children under 21 who are covered by Medicaid.

We specialize in supporting children with complex medical needs—from infants requiring around-the-clock monitoring to young adults transitioning through critical developmental stages.

  • Tracheostomy care and ventilator support
  • G-tube feeding and nutrition management
  • Medication administration and monitoring
  • Skilled wound care and dressing changes

How It Works

Starting Care Is Simple

From your first phone call to the moment a trusted caregiver walks through your door—we guide you every step of the way.

Step 1: Initial Call

Georgia Pediatric Program

Parents Can Become Paid Caregivers

Through the Georgia Pediatric Program (GAPP), eligible parents and family members can receive compensation for providing skilled care to their own medically fragile child. This Medicaid-funded program recognizes the invaluable care you already provide.

GAPP Benefits

  • Get paid to care for your child
  • Professional training provided
  • Ongoing nursing support
  • No cost to your family

Services Covered

  • Tracheostomy & ventilator care
  • G-tube feeding management
  • Medication administration
  • Personal care assistance

Approval typically takes about 30 days

We handle the paperwork and guide you through every step of the GAPP enrollment process.
